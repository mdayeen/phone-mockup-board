# Screen patterns

A catalogue of mobile patterns that recur in well-liked app references (Dribbble, Mobbin and similar), grouped by where they sit on a screen. Mix them so the three options feel genuinely different while following the same flow.

## 1. Headers
- **Greeting + avatar.** A small "Good morning," or a local-language greeting over the name, an avatar or brand initials on the left, one or two round icon buttons on the right (notifications, a channel link).
- **Big question headline.** 26–30px, two lines, very bold: "What's cooking today?", "Where to next?". It sits directly under the greeting.
- **Hero-first.** A photo or video still fills the top 300–340px with a dark-to-clear scrim at the bottom. The headline, a "New" pill and a round play button sit on the photo, and the status bar turns white over it.

## 2. Search
- A grey pill field (about 50px tall, 16px radius) with a search icon, plus a separate square filter button in the primary colour.
- The field with an inline primary "Search" button inside it.
- A search sitting on a white sheet with rounded top corners that overlaps a hero by about 36px.
- In the top bar, as a compact field, when the hero is a headline instead.

## 3. Category navigation
- **Tiles with photo.** Square-ish 60px tiles with a rounded photo and a label under them. The active one gets a primary-colour frame.
- **Photo chips.** Pills with a 22px round photo and a label. The active chip is filled with the primary colour.
- **Story circles.** 88px rings with a photo inside. The active ring is in the primary colour.
- **Colour tiles.** A 2×2 grid of tinted tiles, each with the name, a count and a photo cropped into the corner.
- **Text chips with counts.** For list screens: "Biryani 2".

## 4. Featured / recommended
- **Horizontal action cards.** About 236px wide: photo, two-line title, meta row (time · category), then two buttons side by side (primary "Cook now / Book / Buy" and a soft secondary "Watch / Details").
- **Tall portrait cards.** About 200×250px photos with an overlay badge and a share icon, title and short line below.
- **Wide carousel cards.** 2.4 visible on desktop, 1.2 on a phone, with arrow buttons in the section header.
- **Bento.** One 2×2 tile and two small tiles, a wide utility tile, and a full-width promo banner in the primary colour.
- **Reels.** 9:16 cards with a play button and duration, the title on a bottom scrim. Good for video-led brands.

## 5. Secondary sections
- **"Today" / plan list.** Rows with a thumbnail, title, time and a slot pill on the right (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner, Morning / Evening). Add a "Send to family" share action.
- **Utility rows.** Tools, affiliate items or add-ons: a thumbnail, a name, a disclosure label and a "See it" link.
- **2×2 "of the week" grid.**
- **Collections.** One large image with two stacked small ones and a "39+ items" overlay.

## 6. Bottom bars (give each option a different one)
- **Pill.** The active tab expands to an icon plus label in a tinted pill.
- **Filled icon.** The active icon sits in a filled rounded square in the primary colour, with labels under all icons.
- **Floating.** A detached rounded bar with a shadow, icons only, and the active one filled.

Use tabs the project actually has. Drop Saved/Profile when accounts are out of scope.

## 7. Detail screens
- A hero photo of about 320px with round glass buttons (back, share, channel) at the top.
- A white sheet with rounded top corners overlapping the photo by about 30px. It holds:
  - the title (about 23px, heavy) and a two-line description
  - a stat strip of three tiles (time / level / video length, or size / colour / stock)
  - a segmented control (Ingredients | Steps, Overview | Specs)
- **Stepper row.** "Serves − 4 people +" rescales every quantity live. This is the best single demo moment.
- **List rows.** A rounded border, a small tinted initial or icon tile, the name, and the value right-aligned in tabular figures.
- **Numbered steps.** A tinted number badge followed by the text.
- **Tip callout.** A soft tint background with a coloured label ("Chef's tip", "Ammi ka nuskha").
- **"Goes with" pairs** that open another item in the same phone.
- **Floating primary pill** at the bottom: "Watch video ▶", "Book now", "Add to cart".
- A resilience note when relevant ("If the video doesn't load, every step stays here").

## 8. List / browse screens
- A back button, the title, and a count on the right.
- Search with a filter button.
- Chips with counts.
- A sort select (picks / quickest / longest / A–Z).
- A two-column grid of bordered cards: square photo, badge, title, meta.
- An empty state when a chip has no items.

## 9. Interaction ideas that read well on a recording
- Tapping a category in one phone instantly changes its cards.
- Tapping any card makes the board scroll to the detail phone, which shows that item.
- The stepper changes numbers live.
- The segmented control swaps the content.
- The shade picker recolours all phones at once, which is the moment a client says "that one".
- A WhatsApp or share link that opens a prefilled message (real link, new tab).
